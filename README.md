# Flit

A simple one-tap flapping game. Tap to flap, slip through the gaps and see how far you can get. There are no ads. Sign in with Google (or play as a guest) to earn coins, shop for bird colours, hats, pillar styles and worlds, and compete on an online leaderboard.

## How to play

Flap to keep your bird in the air and fly through the gaps between the pillars. Each pillar you pass is one point. The game speeds up a little as your score grows, and the sky slowly moves from day to night.

**Desktop:** `Space`, `↑` or `W` to flap, or click. `P` or `Esc` pauses.

**Touch:** tap anywhere to flap.

## Coins and shop

Every pillar you pass earns one coin. Spend them in the shop on bird colours, hats, pillar styles and worlds. Guests keep their coins on the same device, and signing in with Google keeps them on any device.

## Leaderboard

Signed-in players appear on a weekly and an all-time leaderboard with their first name and last initial. You can hide yourself from it at any time.

## Sound

The music and sound effects are made in your browser, so there is nothing to download. Use the speaker button to switch music and effects on or off separately. Browsers only allow sound after a tap or key press, so music starts once you first tap or press a key.
